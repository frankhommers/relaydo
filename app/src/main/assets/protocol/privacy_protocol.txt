Relaydo — local privacy notice

This is an independent privacy-focused fork of SmsForwarder by pppscn and contributors.

SMS, call details, contacts and notifications are processed on your device and forwarded only according to the destinations and rules you configure. Forwarding providers receive the content you choose to send; their own policies apply. Saved configurations for providers no longer offered in the editor are retained.

There is no Umeng analytics integration, upstream update check, remote tips feed, phone-number lookup or downloaded FRPC executable loader. FRPC is unavailable. Updates must be installed manually from reviewed builds. Phone-area values are reported as unknown. Online reverse geocoding is disabled; coordinates remain available if you enable location.

At the owner's explicit request, network-change processing uses HTTPS GET https://ip.hommers.it/ to discover the public IP address. This service can observe your IP address and request timing. It receives no SMS content, phone numbers or device identifiers from this request. The response contains one address; this does not discover both address families simultaneously. DNS and Android networking remain in use.

Help/project links open only after you tap them. External websites and their subresources can contact their own services. Tips and this notice are bundled locally. There is no claim that all network traffic is eliminated.

Configuration, credentials and message history are stored locally. Protect your device and exported backups. Debug logging can contain sensitive data; keep it disabled unless needed. Optional forwarding, email, MQTT, HTTP client/server, location and automation capabilities remain powerful: enable only what you understand. OS location providers may use their own network services.

This source review and regression checks are not a comprehensive dependency audit or an on-device packet capture. Review README_PRIVACY.md for verification scope and limitations.
